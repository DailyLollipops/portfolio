## Timekeeper

**Timekeeper** is an **attendance-taking app** with QR-code registration and login, SMS guardian notifications, and report generation - students register once, receive a personal QR code by email, and scan it each time they arrive.

The app closes a real gap in schools that track attendance on paper: it removes manual roll-calling, notifies guardians the moment a student checks in, and automates the reports admins previously generated by hand.

### User flow

1. **Register** - a student fills out the registration form (including guardian contact).
2. **QR delivery** - a personal QR code is emailed to the provided address via SMTP.
3. **Scan to login** - the student scans their QR code at the attendance terminal.
4. **Guardian notified** - an SMS is sent to the recorded guardian contact when the student logs in.
5. **Admin reporting** - the admin can view registered users and attendance records, adjust settings (SMTP credentials, report recipient), and generate reports that are emailed to the configured recipient.

### Architectural overview

A Flutter app organized around distinct service responsibilities rather than a single big controller:

- **Persistence** - local SQLite storage with a seeding step that populates demo data for evaluation
- **Communication services** - email delivery for QR codes, QR-code generation and parsing, and network-time lookups so timestamps don't trust device clocks
- **Screens** - scan, register, home, admin, and report pages, with results tables for attendance and users
- **Widgets** - paginators, dialogs, and loading states reused across screens
- **Configuration** - environment and credential templating keeps SMTP secrets and demo/production mode out of source control; a configurable demo environment populates local dummy data

Registration triggers QR generation and emailing plus guardian-number capture; scanning at the terminal authenticates the student, logs the check-in with a network-verified timestamp, and fires the SMS to the guardian. The admin screen reads the same local store to manage users, adjust SMTP/settings, and email generated reports.

### Feature tour

- **QR-code login** - email-delivered personal QR codes; scanning authenticates the student
- **Automated QR generation** - QRs are generated and sent during registration
- **SMS gateway** - guardian receives an SMS on each login
- **Admin dashboard** - manage users, attendance records, SMTP credentials, and the report recipient
- **Report generation by email** - reports (e.g., per-user or per-date attendance) are generated and sent to a recipient inbox
- **Demo mode** - seeded SQLite data for quick evaluation without a backend
- **Responsive tables & pagination** - attendance and user tables with paging

### Engineering & challenges

- Secure SMTP handling without committing credentials - solved with credential templates and an env-based demo/prod switch
- SMS + email + QR generation all have external dependencies; the service separation isolates them so the UI stays robust
- Reliable attendance timestamps use a network time check rather than trusting device clocks

### Tech stack

- **App:** Flutter, Dart
- **Storage:** SQLite (local)
- **Notifications:** SMS (Telephony) + email (SMTP)
- **QR:** QR-code generation and scanning services
